DATA CLEANING

Data cleaning is the process of detecting and correcting inaccurate, incomplete, duplicated, or inconsistent data.

Missing values are common in datasets. They can be handled by removing records, replacing values with appropriate values, or using statistical methods.

Duplicate records occur when the same data is stored more than once. Removing duplicate records helps improve data quality.

Outliers are data values that are significantly different from other observations. Outliers should be examined carefully because they may represent errors or genuine unusual observations.

Data validation checks whether data follows predefined rules, formats, and constraints. It helps ensure that the data is accurate and consistent.

Data preprocessing may include data cleaning, transformation, and normalization before the data is used for analysis or machine learning.
